# 🏁 F1 Hub 2026: The Official Prediction Scoring Rules

Welcome to the F1 Hub Prediction Game! To keep the competition fierce and rewarding, we have designed a scoring system that favors both accuracy and dedication. Here is how you can earn points and climb the Global Leaderboard.

---

### 1. Position Points (The Accuracy Base)
You earn points by correctly predicting where a driver finishes. We use a hybrid of the official FIA points and custom "Grid Depth" points.

**A. Grand Prix Session (Main Race)**
* **Top 10 Exact Match:** You get the official F1 points: **25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1**.
* **P11 - P22 Exact Match:** For every driver correctly placed in the bottom half (Positions 11 to 22), you earn **2 points**.
* **Steward Bonus:** Correctly predicting a **DNF, DNS, or DSQ** for a driver earns you an extra **+5 points** for that driver.

**B. Sprint Session (Saturday)**
* **Top 8 Exact Match:** You get the official Sprint points: **8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1**.
* **P9 - P22 Exact Match:** Every driver correctly placed outside the Top 8 earns you **1 point**.

---

### 2. Commitment & Precision Bonuses
Filling out the entire grid is the key to winning. These bonuses only apply if your prediction is "Locked" (submitted at least 15 minutes before the session starts).

**A. Completion Rewards**
* **Full Grid Bonus (+25 pts):** Awarded if you fill in all **22 positions** before the deadline.
* **Top 10 Bonus (+10 pts):** Awarded if you fill in at least the first 10 positions.

**B. Perfection Rewards**
* **Perfect Podium (+15 pts):** Awarded if your P1, P2, and P3 predictions for the Grand Prix are all 100% correct.
* **Sprint Podium (+5 pts):** Awarded if your Top 3 for the Sprint session is 100% correct.

---

### 3. Maximum Potential per Weekend
If you are a master predictor and get everything right on a Sprint weekend, here is the math:

* **Max Grand Prix Score:** 175 Points
* **Max Sprint Score:** 75 Points
* **Weekend Maximum:** **250 Points**

---

### 4. Important Rules to Remember
* **The 15-Minute Lock:** All predictions are locked 15 minutes before the lights go out. No last-minute changes!
* **Cancelled Races:** If a race is cancelled (e.g., due to weather), that round scores **0 points** for everyone to keep the championship fair.
* **Live Accuracy:** Your "Season Accuracy %" and total points are updated live in the App Bar as soon as the official results are processed.
